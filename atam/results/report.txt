Findings
  [risk] R-3 The engines do not apply the same data protection by default (Cp1)
      By default EWMA runs its data-quality layer, GARCH-t its jump filter and Realized-CAViaR none, so the
      shared baseline of the portfolio engines differs in 355 forecasts on the synthetic market and 1005 on
      the B3 data (with both protections off: 0 and 266). Every statistical comparison must state the policy
      it runs under.
  [risk] R-4 No common model interface (M1)
      In EWMA and GARCH-t the models are named fields of the engine's result, so a new model touches 5 files
      of EWMA and 5 files of GARCH-t outside the model layer. Realized-CAViaR keeps it to 2 files, its replay
      orchestrator, where the models are selected by position. The monograph's NFR05 (one interface for the
      three models) is not met by the current code.
  [risk] R-5 The target time grid is reachable in one engine only (M2)
      EWMA and GARCH-t set bars in whole minutes and forecast one bar ahead, so the grid of the project
      (5-second sampling, a 5-minute VaR issued every minute) needs code there; Realized-CAViaR implements it
      in its high-frequency mode. The shared 15-minute data keeps every experiment on 15-minute bars, so the
      monograph must state which grid it reports.
  [risk] R-6 The comparison engine has no data-quality layer (R1, R3)
      On the faulty feed 18.0% of Realized-CAViaR's single-stock forecasts move more than 5% (39570
      single-stock forecasts of 15 stocks (353 issued from one feed only, counted as off); >1%: 50.18%; max
      340.6% at 2026-09-21 16:15:00 (XTEC3)): a bad tick enters |R| and RV directly. The garch_t jump filter
      (data_quality.cpp, causal, one stock at a time) could be compiled into realized_caviar like the other
      garch_t sources it reuses, which would protect the single-stock models as well.
  [risk] R-7 Latency is measured at different boundaries (P1)
      EWMA and GARCH-t time a bar cycle of a 15-stock book (fills, data quality, correlations, VaR);
      Realized-CAViaR times the model stage of one instrument in batches of 1000, whose percentiles are of
      batch means. The GARCH-t Monte Carlo revaluation of the option book (7.6 ms per bar at the median) and
      the EWMA confirmation of suspect prices run outside their timers. The numbers are not comparable across
      engines: the cost side of the research question must use the matched model-stage benchmark, where the
      three models share one boundary.
  [risk] R-9 Moderate doses of every recoverable fault still move the VaR (R3)
      On faulty copies of both datasets with missing, duplicated, out-of-order and late rows, halts, frozen
      quotes, bad ticks, corrupt values and early-ending sessions together, EWMA and Realized-CAViaR stay
      above 10% of forecasts more than 5% away from the clean run (EWMA: synthetic 1.8%, b3_real 17.3% of the
      portfolio forecasts more than 5% off, 0.3%, 1.4% not issued; GARCH-t: synthetic 3.3%, b3_real 8.9% of
      the portfolio forecasts more than 5% off, 0.3%, 1.4% not issued; Realized-CAViaR: synthetic 27.3%,
      b3_real 33.1% of the single-stock forecasts (every stock) more than 5% off, 2.4%, 3.5% not issued).
      atam/results/faults/ shows which fault does it, one kind at a time: missing data is the residual problem
      (no protection can recover a print that never arrives).
  [sensitivity] S-1 Missing-data policy of the covariance update (Cp1)
      EWMA keeps a missing asset's variance and shrinks its covariances; GARCH-t imputes the missing return
      from the observed ones. On the B3 data, bars with up to 6 of 15 assets missing make 266 forecasts differ
      (up to 12.6% of the baseline VaR), with both data protections off. Real feeds make this a comparability
      risk the synthetic market cannot show.
  [sensitivity] S-3 The matched comparison does not cover the opening hour (Cp1)
      CAViaR needs one hour of returns of the same session, so Realized-CAViaR scores every model on targets
      starting 11:00-16:45 only. The most volatile bars of the day (the opening hour) are outside the
      comparison, so its conclusions about the models hold from 11:00 on.
  [sensitivity] S-4 Reuse by compiling the other modules' sources (Cp1, M1)
      Realized-CAViaR compiles 9 source files of ewma and garch_t (loader, EWMA, GARCH, backtest). It
      guarantees the same conventions and estimators in the comparison, but couples the modules with no
      interface or version: a change in ewma or garch_t silently changes the comparison engine. atam/run.bat
      rebuilds it before every evaluation, so a rerun after such a change is the safeguard.
  [sensitivity] S-6 An unprinted bar widens the next forecast on purpose (R1)
      On the faulty feed 1.46% of the GARCH-t forecasts move more than 5% from the clean-feed ones, but 38 of
      them are issued at or right after a bar in which a stock did not print; without those the share is
      0.23%. The engine marks a stock that skips a bar at its last print, so the P&L of its next bar spans two
      bars, and it adds the variance of the unprinted bar to that forecast: the clean-feed VaR covers one bar,
      the faulty-feed one covers the P&L it is actually scored on. R1 counts this deliberate response as a
      deviation; the threshold would be met if the scenario compared forecasts of the same P&L horizon.
  [sensitivity] S-7 The option revaluation slows down the bar update it is not part of (P1)
      With the option book, the Monte Carlo revaluation runs on the same thread between two bar updates (the
      revaluation itself takes 7.6 ms per bar at the median) and evicts the engine's data from the processor
      caches, so the next update starts cold: GARCH-t p99 18.4 us with the book, 2.6 us without it (p50 3.8 vs
      1.3 us). Running the revaluation on another core after the VaR is published would keep the bar update at
      its own cost.
  [trade-off] T-1 Data-quality layer: robustness vs reaction to genuine shocks (R1, R2, P1)
      With the layer, 0.03% of the faulty-feed forecasts stray more than 5% from the clean-feed ones (without
      it: 10.01%). The price is paid on genuine moves: at a shock bar the VaR moves up to 2.0% away from the
      unprotected one, for 1 bar, because the suspect stock keeps its last price and a winsorized return until
      the next bar confirms the move. Timed latency cost: none measurable (median per bar 1.30 us with the
      layer, 1.20 us without, inside the run-to-run spread). Its confirmation step runs before the timer, so
      part of its cost is not in this figure.
  [trade-off] T-2 GARCH-t: a stateful jump filter against stateless clipping (R1, R2, R3)
      On the faulty feed, 25.6% of the unprotected GARCH-t forecasts stray more than 5%; clipping shocks at 6
      sd leaves 16.0% and the jump filter (default) 1.46%. With every recoverable fault together (R3): 66.7%
      unprotected, 38.0% clipped, 8.9% filtered. At the genuine shocks of the clean feed, the lowest ratio to
      the unprotected VaR is 0.973 with clipping and 0.998 with the filter: the filter delays a move confined
      to one stock by one bar, then lets it in at full size, while clipping shrinks every large shock for as
      long as it is in the variance.
  [non-risk] N1 No look-ahead in the data path (C1)
      Replacing every price after a cut time leaves all earlier forecasts and calibrations unchanged in EWMA,
      GARCH-t and Realized-CAViaR (1,236,467 cells over three cuts); the same test changes 100% of the later
      forecasts (lowest engine), so it would have caught a leak.
  [non-risk] N2 Replays are deterministic (Rp1)
      Three runs of EWMA, GARCH-t and Realized-CAViaR produce identical output files apart from timing columns
      (1,008,650 cells compared), including the multithreaded GARCH-t refits, the Monte Carlo option
      revaluation and the multi-start CAViaR fits.
  [non-risk] N3 The portfolio engines read the market identically on clean data (Cp1)
      With both data protections off, the EWMA(0.94) baseline that EWMA and GARCH-t both compute is identical
      forecast by forecast on the synthetic market: the two pipelines read, align and return the market in the
      same way.
  [non-risk] N5 The model comparison runs on the shared market and on matched targets (Cp1)
      Realized-CAViaR reads the market with the repository's shared convention (synthetic: 2638 of 2638 target
      returns equal the shared convention; b3_real: 1120 of 1120 target returns equal the shared convention;
      the three models share one target record per forecast by construction). It is the engine in which the
      research question can be answered fairly.
  [non-risk] N4 Latency and recalibration are far below the bar interval (P1, P2, P3)
      Per-bar updates of the portfolio engines take microseconds (EWMA p99 11.0 us, GARCH-t p99 18.4 us;
      Realized-CAViaR updates in 18 ns at the model stage) and the daily refit at most EWMA 8 ms, GARCH-t 118
      ms, Realized-CAViaR 41 ms, against 15-minute bars: at this scale performance does not constrain the
      designs. Timings vary between sessions on the same machine (up to a factor of two), so verdicts near a
      threshold are marked PARTIAL.
  [non-risk] N6 The main model is the cheapest one under a common boundary (P1, P4)
      In the matched model-stage benchmark (Realized-CAViaR 18 ns / 40 B, GARCH-t 24 ns / 280 B, EWMA-N 27 ns
      / 240 B, EWMA-t 27 ns / 248 B) Realized-CAViaR has the lowest update cost and the smallest state: the
      computational side of the research question does not penalize it.
