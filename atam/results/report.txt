Findings
  [risk] R-8 A shock followed by a halt never reaches the CAViaR features (R2)
      Realized-CAViaR builds its features from four consecutive bars of the session and skips incomplete
      windows. The scripted market crash (market crash) is followed by a two-bar halt, so no window contains
      it: the forecasts of that session never see the crash, while the matched baselines of the same engine,
      which take every printed bar, do. A halt right after a crash is exactly when the VaR matters most.
  [risk] R-1 The default estimation window depends on how much data follows (C1)
      EWMA, GARCH-t and Realized-CAViaR set the estimation window to half of the sessions in the file (at most
      60). Cut the feed at T, as a live feed is, and earlier forecasts change (EWMA: 42,726 values and rows,
      GARCH-t: 39,607 values and rows, Realized-CAViaR: 11,368 values and rows). With --window-days fixed
      these changes vanish (the 2 rows of EWMA that remain are R-2): the window must be configuration, never
      derived from the input length.
  [risk] R-2 The end of the data is taken as the end of the session (C1)
      The portfolio replays mark the last bar of the file as a session close (batch assumption: ewma
      replay.cpp:78, garch_t replay.cpp:94). With a fixed window, a feed cut at the first bar of a session
      still changes 2 rows in EWMA: the pending overnight forecast is replaced by a record whose target is its
      own issue time. A streaming engine must take the session close from the calendar.
  [risk] R-3 The engines do not apply the same data-quality policy by default (Cp1)
      EWMA runs its data-quality layer by default and GARCH-t and Realized-CAViaR have none, so the shared
      baseline of the portfolio engines differs in 6 forecasts on the synthetic market and 320 on the B3 data.
      Every statistical comparison must run the engines under one declared policy.
  [risk] R-4 No common model interface (M1)
      None of the engines has a model interface. In EWMA and GARCH-t the models are named fields of the
      engine's result, so a new model touches 5 files of EWMA and 5 files of GARCH-t outside the model layer.
      Realized-CAViaR keeps it to 2 files, its replay orchestrator, where the models are selected by position.
      The monograph's NFR05 (one interface for the three models) is not met by the current code.
  [risk] R-5 The target time grid is reachable in one engine only (M2)
      EWMA and GARCH-t set bars in whole minutes and forecast one bar ahead, so the grid of the project
      (5-second sampling, a 5-minute VaR issued every minute) needs code there; Realized-CAViaR implements it
      in its high-frequency mode. The shared 15-minute data keeps every experiment on 15-minute bars, so the
      monograph must state which grid it reports.
  [risk] R-6 The comparison engine has no data-quality layer (R1)
      On the faulty feed 24.7% of Realized-CAViaR's single-stock forecasts move more than 5% (32340
      single-stock forecasts of 15 stocks (685 issued from one feed only, counted as off); >1%: 59.80%; max
      532.3% at 2026-07-08 14:45:00 (XPET3)): a bad tick enters |R| and RV directly. Moving the EWMA
      data-quality rule into the shared loader (garch_t market_data, compiled by all engines) would protect
      every model at once.
  [risk] R-7 Latency is measured at different boundaries (P1)
      EWMA and GARCH-t time a bar cycle of a 15-stock book (fills, data quality, correlations, VaR);
      Realized-CAViaR times the model stage of one instrument in batches of 1000, whose percentiles are of
      batch means. The GARCH-t Monte Carlo revaluation of the option book (7.1 ms per bar at the median) and
      the EWMA confirmation of suspect prices run outside their timers. The numbers are not comparable across
      engines: the cost side of the research question must use the matched model-stage benchmark, where the
      three models share one boundary.
  [sensitivity] S-1 Missing-data policy of the covariance update (Cp1)
      EWMA keeps a missing asset's variance and shrinks its covariances; GARCH-t imputes the missing return
      from the observed ones. On the B3 data, bars with up to 6 of 15 assets missing make 139 forecasts differ
      (up to 12.6% of the baseline VaR), with the same data-quality policy. Real feeds make this a
      comparability risk the synthetic market cannot show.
  [sensitivity] S-2 Tail recalibration on fills runs inside the bar cycle (P1)
      GARCH-t recalibrates its tail model when a fill changes the book, inside the timed bar update: those
      bars take up to 1,332 us against a median of 4.2 us (without fill bars the maximum is 76 us). Moving it
      out of the critical path would flatten the latency tail.
  [sensitivity] S-3 The matched comparison does not cover the opening hour (Cp1)
      CAViaR needs one hour of returns of the same session, so Realized-CAViaR scores every model on targets
      starting 11:00-16:45 only. The most volatile bars of the day (the opening hour) are outside the
      comparison, so its conclusions about the models hold from 11:00 on.
  [sensitivity] S-4 Reuse by compiling the other modules' sources (Cp1, M1)
      Realized-CAViaR compiles 9 source files of ewma and garch_t (loader, EWMA, GARCH, backtest). It
      guarantees the same conventions and estimators in the comparison, but couples the modules with no
      interface or version: a change in ewma or garch_t silently changes the comparison engine. atam/run.bat
      rebuilds it before every evaluation, so a rerun after such a change is the safeguard.
  [trade-off] T-1 Data-quality layer: robustness vs reaction to genuine shocks (R1, R2, P1)
      With the layer, 0.04% of the faulty-feed forecasts stray more than 5% from the clean-feed ones (without
      it: 8.34%). The price is paid on genuine moves: at a shock bar the VaR moves up to 2.1% away from the
      unprotected one, for 1 bar, because the suspect stock keeps its last price and a winsorized return until
      the next bar confirms the move. Timed latency cost: none measurable (median per bar 1.30 us with the
      layer, 1.20 us without, inside the run-to-run spread). Its confirmation step runs before the timer, so
      part of its cost is not in this figure.
  [trade-off] T-2 Stateless clipping: half the protection, and it dampens real shocks (R1, R2)
      Clipping standardized shocks at 6 sd cuts the faulty-feed damage of GARCH-t from 26.4% to 13.6% of
      forecasts, but it also clips genuine shocks (lowest VaR ratio at a shock 0.962, 27 bars affected). In
      these runs the stateful rule of the EWMA engine, which confirms a suspect move with the next bar, does
      better on both sides; the engines also differ in model, so this is indicative (porting the rule to
      GARCH-t would settle it).
  [non-risk] N1 No look-ahead in the data path (C1)
      Replacing every price after a cut time leaves all earlier forecasts and calibrations unchanged in EWMA,
      GARCH-t and Realized-CAViaR (982,027 cells over three cuts); the same test changes 100% of the later
      forecasts (lowest engine), so it would have caught a leak.
  [non-risk] N2 Replays are deterministic (Rp1)
      Three runs of EWMA, GARCH-t and Realized-CAViaR produce identical output files apart from timing columns
      (726,392 cells compared), including the multithreaded GARCH-t refits, the Monte Carlo option revaluation
      and the multi-start CAViaR fits.
  [non-risk] N3 The portfolio engines read the market identically on clean data (Cp1)
      With the same data-quality policy, the EWMA(0.94) baseline that EWMA and GARCH-t both compute is
      identical forecast by forecast on the synthetic market: the two pipelines read, align and return the
      market in the same way.
  [non-risk] N5 The model comparison runs on the shared market and on matched targets (Cp1)
      Realized-CAViaR reads the market with the repository's shared convention (synthetic: 2156 of 2156 target
      returns equal the shared convention; b3_real: 720 of 720 target returns equal the shared convention; the
      three models share one target record per forecast by construction). It is the engine in which the
      research question can be answered fairly.
  [non-risk] N4 Latency and recalibration are far below the bar interval (P1, P2, P3)
      Per-bar updates of the portfolio engines take microseconds (EWMA p99 5.2 us, GARCH-t p99 10.0 us;
      Realized-CAViaR updates in 27 ns at the model stage) and the daily refit at most EWMA 8 ms, GARCH-t 122
      ms, Realized-CAViaR 76 ms, against 15-minute bars: at this scale performance does not constrain the
      designs. Timings vary by a factor of two between sessions on the reference laptop, so verdicts near a
      threshold are marked PARTIAL.
  [non-risk] N6 The main model is the cheapest one under a common boundary (P1, P4)
      In the matched model-stage benchmark (Realized-CAViaR 27 ns / 40 B, GARCH-t 40 ns / 280 B, EWMA-N 45 ns
      / 240 B) Realized-CAViaR has the lowest update cost and the smallest state: the computational side of
      the research question does not penalize it.
